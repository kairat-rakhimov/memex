How · Flow 2Hypothesis sweep
14 / 15

A focused sweep — and duds get rejected twice.

Two agents reading the real prose throw work out: the Explorer bails on fake-contested seeds, and VerifyEvidence kills any candidate whose premises aren’t in the sources. Only genuinely grounded leads survive to the report.
